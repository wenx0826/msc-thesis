Welcome to the Document-Based Process Model Explorer, or DBPME. If you've ever struggled to turn lengthy standard operating procedures or manuals into clear, verifiable process models, DBPME is built for you. It seamlessly combines document analysis, AI-assisted extraction, and process modeling—all in one environment.

When you launch the application, you'll start at the Dashboard. Here, you get a clean overview of your workspaces, including your recent projects and high-level statistics covering the total documents and models stored in your system.

Let’s create your first workflow. Click 'New Project', give it a descriptive name—like 'HR Onboarding 2026'—and save. Projects act as your dedicated workspace to safely organize the specific documents and models associated with a business process. Once created, simply click into the project to get started.

Before we can generate a model, we need our source material. Navigate to the Documents tab and click 'Upload'. You can upload standard text or HTML files—typically your SOPs, operational manuals, or internal policy guidelines. In seconds, your document is loaded securely into the project."

Click your document to enter the Workspace View. This is where the magic happens. You can read your document on one side while building your model on the other. DBPME allows you to highlight specific process-related text and use LLM assistance to generate workflow elements instantly. The best part? Traceability. Every generated model element remains permanently linked to the exact sentence or paragraph it came from, ensuring your models are always verifiable.

Whether you are mapping out automated software release processes or updating HR pipelines, DBPME grounds your process architecture in reality. Upload your first document and start modeling today.
