Credit Check

The credit check is performed after the order has been validated and before it is released for fulfillment.

The process typically consists of several stages, from automated evaluation to potential manual review.

Credit evaluation

When the order reaches this stage, the system evaluates its financial impact.

The total order value is compared against the customer’s available credit, taking into account existing exposure such as open invoices and unpaid balances.

If the calculated exposure remains within the allowed limit, the order can proceed without further action.

Detection of credit issues

If issues are detected, the order is flagged by the system.

If the order exceeds the available credit or violates agreed payment conditions, it is placed on hold and cannot proceed further in the process.

Review process

Orders that are placed on hold are then assigned for review by the finance team.

During this step, the financial situation of the customer is assessed in more detail.

This may involve evaluating payment history or deciding whether an exception can be granted.

Decision and release

After the review has been completed, a decision is made regarding the order.

Approved orders are released and continue to the next step of processing.

Rejected orders remain blocked and are not processed further.

Notes

In most cases, the credit check is handled automatically by the system.

Manual review is typically only required when predefined limits are exceeded.
