
> The system’s data is organised around a set of core entities that capture documents, process models, and their relationships. Figure X illustrates the main entities and their associations in the database schema.

> Documents are stored together with their corresponding versions, allowing users to maintain and update document content over time. Similarly, process models are represented with versioned structures, supporting iterative refinement and regeneration.

>   

> A key aspect of the design is the linkage between text selections and process model elements. Selected text segments are associated with specific model versions, enabling traceability between source content and generated models.

>   

> In addition, the system supports hierarchical relationships between models through subprocess linking, allowing models to be organised into multi-level structures.
