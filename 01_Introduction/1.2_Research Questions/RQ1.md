**~~What are the key approaches, tools, and principal challenges in the domain of document-based business process modelling, and what opportunities for innovation does the era of Generative AI present?~~**
This foundational question aims to situate the project within the current academic and technological landscape. It involves a critical review of existing process modeling tools and the paradigm shift introduced by Generative AI. The core of this investigation is to identify the principal challenges of this new technology—specifically its limitations regarding traceability and user trust—and thereby define the opportunity for innovation that this thesis will address.

**What challenges arise when modelling business processes from documents, particularly in integrating document interpretation and process modelling?**

This research question aims to frame the fundamental challenges associated with modelling business processes from textual documents. In such scenarios, users are required to interpret unstructured or semi-structured information and translate it into formal process models, which introduces a gap between document understanding and model construction.

This process is often fragmented, as document analysis and process modelling are typically performed in separate environments, requiring users to switch between tools and manually transfer information. As a result, this separation can increase cognitive effort, reduce efficiency, and make it difficult to maintain clear traceability between source documents and the resulting process models.

In this context, the research question focuses on understanding how these challenges emerge, particularly with regard to the integration of document interpretation and process modelling. Addressing these challenges provides the foundation for the design of a system that supports a more integrated and coherent modelling workflow.
