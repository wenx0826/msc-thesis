> This research question focuses on the design of the proposed system and aims to identify how document-based process modeling can be effectively supported through an integrated workflow. The motivation behind this question lies in the fragmented nature of existing approaches, where document analysis and process modeling are often performed in separate tools, leading to inefficiencies and increased cognitive load for users.

> In this context, the question investigates how different functionalities—such as document interaction, model generation, and the management of relationships between models and their underlying textual sources—can be integrated into a coherent environment. The goal is to support users not only in completing modeling tasks effectively, but also in interacting with the system in a usable and intuitive manner.

> Rather than evaluating isolated features, this question emphasises the overall design of the system, particularly how the integration of functionalities contributes to a ==seamless workflow== for document-based process modeling. The answer to this research question is reflected in the design of the proposed system, including its key components and interaction mechanisms.


This research question focuses on the design of the proposed integrated system and investigates how document-based process modeling can be effectively supported by integrating multiple functionalities within a single system.
