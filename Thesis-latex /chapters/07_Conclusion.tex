\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this work, we propose and develop a process modeling tool, DBPM. It provides a new modeling workflow for process modeling from documents, which allows users to upload documents, selects passages from the documents and auto-generate process models. The tool also maintains a clear link between the text and the generated model, and allows users to establish process-subprocess relationships between processes and subprocesses. We evaluate the tool with 4 experts from both academia and industry, and the results show that the tool is usable and intuitive, and capable of supporting document-based process modeling tasks within a unified environment.
It provides a new interactive approach between the source text and the its corresponding model.
