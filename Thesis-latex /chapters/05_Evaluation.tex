\section{Evaluation Setup}

A qualitative evaluation was conducted with four expert users to gain insights into the usability and potential of the proposed system. The evaluation was carried out in the form of semi-structured interviews.

At the beginning of the evaluation, participants received a brief introduction to the system to familiarise themselves with its main functionalities. The tasks included a simple example designed for participants to explore the system, followed by a more realistic process modelling task based on a real-world document. If time permitted, they could also use the system with their own documents.

After completing the tasks, participants were asked open-ended questions about what they liked and disliked about the system, as well as potential improvements and additional features. Additionally, they were asked whether they would consider using the system in the future to model processes from documents and to explain their reasoning. The interviews lasted approximately 40 minutes on average.
