\chapter*{Abstract}
\noindent
Business processes are critical organizational assets; however, a large portion of process knowledge is stored in unstructured formats such as Word documents and PDF files, making the manual transformation into structured process models time-consuming and error-prone. Large language models (LLMs) have shown strong potential in supporting more efficient process modeling, but they cannot fully replace human experts, especially in domain-specific scenarios. Hybrid human–LLM process modeling is therefore a promising approach to leverage the strengths of both and compensate for their respective weaknesses. However, existing tools and approaches still mainly focus on model generation capabilities and output quality, which makes them less suitable for the scenario of creating process models directly from documents. To address this limitation, this work proposes and develops an integrated web-based tool that supports process modeling from documents through a more seamless, efficient, and user-friendly workflow. The system integrates document management, LLM-based model generation, visual traceability between text and models, subprocess relationship support, and model version control. A user evaluation with four process modeling experts shows that the system is intuitive, usable, and valuable, particularly in scenarios involving multiple models and documents.

\textit{\textbf{Keywords: } Business Process Management, Process Modeling}
