> Process modeling from textual documents is a time-consuming and complex task that requires both domain knowledge and modeling expertise. In this paper, we present a system that leverages large language models to support the automatic generation and iterative refinement of process models from textual descriptions. The system enables users to upload documents, generate initial models, and interactively improve them while maintaining traceability between text and model elements.

>   

> We conducted a qualitative evaluation with four domain experts to assess the usability and potential of the system. The results indicate that the system is intuitive to use and effective in producing initial process models, particularly for early-stage exploration. Participants highlighted the value of versioning, traceability, and visualization features, while also identifying limitations related to interaction clarity, system transparency, and document handling.

>   

> Overall, our findings suggest that LLM-based approaches have strong potential for supporting document-driven process modeling. However, further work is needed to improve usability, explainability, and integration with document understanding techniques.
